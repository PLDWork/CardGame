This is the enemy deck.
Each end of turn, every enemy moves one space to the right.
You lose if there is an enemy on space 5 when you end your turn.
You win if you defeat every enemy.

This is your deck.
Each turn, you can play all your cards in the order that you choose.
Using the Play All button will play all your cards from left to right.
When you end your turn, all cards played and remaining in your hand will be discarded and your will draw 5 new cards.
If you have to draw a card and your deck is empty, your discard pile is shuffle to make a new deck.

This is the shop row.
Buy new cards to make your deck stronger.
Cards you buy to into your discard.
